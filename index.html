Why Open Graph & Schema Markup Are Critical for Modern SEO

When users share URLs on platforms like Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Slack, web scrapers fetch Open Graph (og:) and Twitter Card meta tags to render rich visual link cards. Pages with rich previews achieve up to 3x higher click-through rates (CTR).

Essential Open Graph Tags:

  • og:title - Headline of the content (optimal length 40-60 characters).
  • og:description - 2-3 sentence summary (optimal length 120-160 characters).
  • og:image - High-resolution image (recommended dimension 1200 x 630 pixels).
  • og:url - Canonical permalink URL.
